Privacy Policy

Gapsy: Color Slide Puzzle · Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy describes how Gapsy (previously Color Slide), an Android puzzle game published by Muhammad Azeem Ullah, handles information. Data practices depend on the version and features in use. This policy covers the game and these policy pages.

No game account required

Gapsy does not require an account, Facebook Login, or a player profile. The game does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise GPS location. If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send.

Local game data

Progress, stars, coins, move balances, settings and purchase entitlements are stored on your device. Device backup services may also preserve app data according to your device settings. Gameplay analytics do not upload your full saved game. Clearing app storage removes local progress and settings; restoration from backups is controlled by your device and backup provider.

Advertising: Google AdMob

Gapsy uses Google AdMob to display ads. The advertising SDK may collect advertising and other device identifiers, IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and app information, ad views and interactions, and diagnostic information. These are used for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. We may receive advertising performance reports.

Advertising consent and privacy choices are offered where supported or required. You can also manage your advertising ID through Android settings; menu names vary by device. The game's Usage analytics switch does not replace AdMob's separate privacy controls. See Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob privacy information.

Usage analytics: Google Analytics for Firebase

When configured and enabled, Firebase measures app opens, sessions, engagement, returning players, tutorial progress, level starts and outcomes, scores, moves used, level duration, game modes, ad results and reminder interactions. Firebase also processes app-instance identifiers and device/app and network information. We use these reports to understand difficulty, improve the game and measure advertising campaigns. We do not assign an account-based user ID or send names, email addresses, friend challenge codes, payment-card details, or complete saved games in gameplay events.

Firebase Analytics advertising-ID collection and advertising-personalization signals are disabled in the current Android configuration. This does not disable AdMob's separate advertising features. See Firebase privacy information.

Campaign measurement: Meta App Events

When configured and enabled, the Meta SDK sends app activity such as activation, tutorial progress, levels started or achieved, and other gameplay events to Meta to measure Facebook and Instagram campaigns. It also processes SDK-generated identifiers, device/app and network information, and install-referrer information where available. Depending on SDK and Meta app settings, automatic events can include sessions and Google Play purchase events. No Facebook login is required by Gapsy.

Meta advertising-ID collection, automatic screen metadata collection and product-improvement collection are disabled in the current configuration. We do not send names, contact lists, email addresses, challenge codes or payment-card details in gameplay events. See Meta's Privacy Policy.

Your analytics choice

In Gapsy, open Settings and turn off Usage analytics to stop future Firebase and Meta analytics collection controlled by the app. Already queued or transmitted data may still be processed. Turning this setting off does not delete previously collected information. AdMob has separate controls.

Purchases and reminders

Google Play Billing processes purchases. The game receives purchase status, product and transaction information needed to grant or restore entitlements; it does not receive your card or bank details. Google retains its purchase records and handles payment and refund requests. Optional local reminders use Android notification permissions and locally stored scheduling settings. Reminder interactions may be included in usage analytics.

Support and website hosting

If you contact us, we use your message and contact details to respond and handle your request. Do not send passwords or payment-card details. These public policy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical request information such as IP addresses and browser details. These pages contain no added analytics scripts, advertising scripts or submission forms. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Retention, sharing and security

Local game information remains until it is cleared or otherwise removed, subject to device backups. Analytics and advertising information is retained according to the relevant service settings, purposes and provider policies; there is no single retention period for all these services. Support correspondence is kept as needed to resolve requests and meet applicable obligations. Some records may need to be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention or transaction purposes.

Google, Meta and the website hosting provider may process information outside your country. We use their services for the purposes explained above. We do not publish identifiable player information. We use reasonable safeguards, but no storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your rights and deletion requests

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict use of personal information, withdraw consent, or complain to a relevant authority. Email us using the contact below. Since Gapsy has no accounts, we may need limited additional information to locate data and verify a request. We will explain any limits, applicable retention requirements or inability to identify particular records. See our data-deletion instructions for local deletion and requests concerning previously collected data.

Children

Gapsy is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can investigate and address it.

Updates

We may update this policy as the game or its data practices change. The date above identifies the latest version.
