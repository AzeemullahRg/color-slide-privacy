Data Deletion Instructions

Gapsy: Color Slide Puzzle · Last updated: 3 November 2026

You can remove Gapsy's local game data and request deletion of personal information associated with the game. Gapsy has no account-registration or Facebook-login system, so there is no game account to close.

1. Stop future usage analytics

Open Gapsy → Settings → Usage analytics and turn it off. This controls future Firebase and Meta analytics collection by the app. It does not delete historical data, recall events already sent or queued, or change AdMob's separate advertising controls.

2. Delete game data on your Android device

  1. Open Android Settings → Apps → Gapsy → Storage (or Storage & cache).
  2. Select Clear storage / Clear data and confirm.
  3. You may also uninstall Gapsy if you no longer want to use it.

This removes local progress, stars, coins, move balances and settings. Menu names vary by device. Check your device's backup settings if you also want to remove backed-up app data; a later restore may bring back previously saved information. Clearing storage does not remove Google Play purchase records or automatically refund purchases.

3. Request deletion of previously collected data

Email azeemzafar527@gmail.com with the subject Gapsy data deletion request.

State that the request concerns Gapsy and describe what you want deleted, such as support correspondence or identifiable analytics data. To help us locate information, you may include your app version, approximate usage dates and device model. An email address alone does not identify anonymous game installations. We may ask for limited additional information needed to verify and process your request. Do not send passwords, App Secrets, access tokens, payment-card details or identity documents unless a separately explained, necessary verification process requires them.

What can be removed and what may remain

  • Local game data: removed using the device steps above; we cannot remotely clear your device.
  • Support messages and identifiable records under our control: we review and delete eligible information we can locate, subject to applicable obligations.
  • Firebase and Meta analytics: uninstalling does not erase provider-held records. We will assess what can be identified and deleted through the available provider processes, and explain any limitations. Data that has been aggregated or cannot be linked to your installation may not be identifiable as your record.
  • Advertising and purchase records: Google and Meta may retain information under their own policies and applicable requirements. Google Play payments and refunds are handled through Google Play; deleting game data does not cancel a subscription, if you have one. Manage subscriptions in Google Play.
  • Required retention: information needed for legal, security, fraud-prevention or transaction obligations may be retained for those purposes. We will explain relevant limits when responding.

Request handling

We will review your request and respond within applicable legal deadlines. Completion depends on verifying and locating the relevant information and on any applicable retention requirements. There is no automatic promise that all third-party records will be deleted immediately.

For provider-specific controls, see Google's Privacy Policy and Meta's Privacy Policy. For the complete description of Gapsy's data practices, read our Privacy Policy.
